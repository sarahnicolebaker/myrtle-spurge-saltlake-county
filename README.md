# myrtle-spurge-saltlake-county
This repository hosts a small GIS analysis of where myrtle spurge (Eurphorbia myrsinites) has been observed in Salt Lake County. 

### Question:
At which elevation, slope, and aspect has myrtle spurge been observed in Salt Lake County? Which areas are most likely to see myrtle spurge growth, based on historical observations?

Myrtle spurge has been classified as a noxious weed in Salt Lake County due to both its history of crowding out native vegetation and its toxic sap, which causes blistering and skin irritation[1]. 

### Data: 
- Myrtle spurge observations
  - Source: iNaturalist export as of 10.30.2026
  - Notes on observation selection
    - Research grade
    - Wild observations
    - Location: Salt Lake County
- Elevation
  - Source: Utah Geospatial Resource Center (UGRC)
- County boundary
  - Source: US Census Bureau cartographic boundary file
